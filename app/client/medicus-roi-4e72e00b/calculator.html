Basis and limits
  • Source

    Built from the measured impact of Medicus at a live NHS practice (the reference practice) with 8 FTE GPs, 3 FTE practice nurses and 0.5 FTE of admin time released across 12,310 registered patients. Time saved per login and per session, and the admin time released, are that practice's own figures.

  • Staffing presets

    Reference practice ratios give one GP FTE per 1,539 patients and one practice nurse FTE per 4,103. The national average preset uses NHS England's general practice workforce figures for February 2026 (28,895 FTE fully qualified GPs and 16,728 FTE nurses) against 63,789,531 registered patients, or about 2,210 patients per GP and 3,810 per nurse. The reference practice is better staffed than average, so its ratios give the higher figure. NHS England counts an FTE as 37.5 hours; the model follows the reference practice in counting one as four clinical days.

  • Appointments

    Clinical capacity is clinical hours released divided by a ten-minute appointment. It shows the scale of the time, not a promise that every hour becomes an appointment; the reference practice's own view is that the larger benefit is a calmer working day.

  • Valuation

    Clinical hours are valued at the session rate divided by session length (a £400 GP session over four hours gives £100 an hour). Admin time uses a loaded hourly cost of £15: the National Living Wage rose to £12.71 in April 2026, and employer National Insurance and pension take it to about £15. The reference practice used £12.50. The model assumes 52 working weeks; 46 is more cautious.

  • Possible overlap

    Login time and session slowdowns are counted separately, as the reference practice counted them. Some of the login saving may already sit inside the slowdown figure, so the two together should be read as an upper estimate.

  • Native functions and add-ons

    The reference practice used Medicus's built-in functions to retire AccuRx, Docman, Docman Connect and ICE, and cut its texting spend by defaulting to email. The slide gave no costs. Patient messaging is estimated at £0.53 a patient a year, the AccuRx Bronze toolkit list price on G-Cloud 14 for lists under 15,000 (ex VAT). Online consultation is estimated at £0.45 a patient, the bottom of the £0.45 to £0.90 range OneAdvanced lists for PATCHS on G-Cloud 14; the equivalent AccuRx step from Bronze to Silver is £0.32. Clinical templates and searches use Ardens Starter on G-Cloud 15 at 29p a patient with a £1,119 minimum, excluding the one-off setup fee. Self check-in and patient calling screens use Engage Health Systems' G-Cloud 15 software prices of £249 and £149 a screen, assuming one screen per 8,000 patients and excluding hardware, so a practice with kiosks from Jayex or similar will usually pay more. Patient registration add-ons such as Healthtech-1 price by population band on request, so they start at zero. SMS is estimated at £0.50 a patient a year, around 17 message fragments at Accurx's 2.99p list rate; North East and North Cumbria ICB treats practices above £60 per 1,000 patients a month (£0.72 a patient a year) as high spenders. The panic button, which Medicus includes as standard, is estimated from Little Green Button's G-Cloud 15 price per device per month (£4.16 for up to 10 devices, £3.30 up to 25, £2.50 up to 50, £2.00 up to 200, billed annually), assuming one device per 500 patients; EDGEALERT lists £195 a practice site a year for up to 100 devices on G-Cloud 15, so a practice on that product should enter £195. Document scanning, referral letters, ICE ordering and booking add-ons have no public price and are often funded by the ICB, the sending trust or the pathology provider, so they start at zero. Where an add-on is commissioned centrally the saving belongs to the commissioner, and should be presented that way.
